Your ddeploy Pro key is in the email Dodo Payments just sent you (check spam if it isn't there within a minute). Two ways to activate:

  1. Paste the key here and click Activate. ddeploy opens and activates itself.
    Activate in ddeploy

    Needs ddeploy installed. Download it if you haven't yet, then come back to this page.

  2. Or inside the app: ddeploy → Settings → License → paste the key → Activate.

One key works on one Mac. Moving to a new Mac? Deactivate in Settings, License on the old one first. Lost the email? Reply to your Dodo receipt or write to the support address on the receipt and it'll be resent.
