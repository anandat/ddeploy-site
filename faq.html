Questions & answers

Is ddeploy really free?

Scanning is free forever: every screen, every size, the explorer, System Data, Ask AI. Your first full clean is free as well, so you watch the space come back before paying anything. After that, cleaning needs Pro, $9.99 once.

Does it work offline?

Completely. Scanning and cleaning happen 100% on your Mac, nothing is uploaded, ever. There's no account and no telemetry. Three things touch the network, all optional: the once-a-day update check (a plain request that carries only the app and macOS version; switch it off in Settings and the app is fully offline), license activation if you buy Pro, and the Ask AI button, which opens your own browser.

Is ddeploy safe to use?

Yes, it's the whole design. ddeploy only deletes the contents of well-known cache locations, moves your own files (backups, installers, large files, leftovers) to the Trash where they're recoverable, keeps a protected list it never touches, and shows a confirmation listing what happens before cleaning anything. Unsure about an item? Right-click it and ask Claude or ChatGPT about that exact file.

How do I install it?

Open the DMG, drag ddeploy to Applications, and open it. The app is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other Mac app. For full results (iPhone backups, Messages, protected folders) grant Full Disk Access when it asks.

What's free, what's paid?

Scanning is free forever, every screen, every size, the drill-down explorer, the System Data breakdown, the memory view, and Ask AI. Your first clean is free too. ddeploy Pro ($9.99, one time) unlocks unlimited cleaning after that, on one Mac, for life.

Is it a subscription?

No. One purchase, yours for life. No account either, your license key arrives by email and activates in Settings.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

macOS protects some folders (iOS backups, Messages data, parts of Library). Granting Full Disk Access in System Settings lets ddeploy measure and clean them. Without it, the app still works, those boxes just read smaller than reality.

What actually happens after I clean caches?

They rebuild on demand: Docker re-pulls images when you use them, Xcode re-derives build data on the next build, npm and pip re-download what a project needs. You trade disk space now for a slightly slower first build later. Model libraries are different, a 40 GB Ollama model is a real re-download, which is why models are never preselected and can be deleted one at a time instead.

Does ddeploy collect any data?

No telemetry, no analytics, no account, no ads. The only network calls are license activation (if you buy Pro) and the Ask AI button, which opens your own browser with your own AI account.
